## 1. Introduction

Frosthoot Studio ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data the **Waktu Shalat** application ("App") collects, how we use it, and your rights over that data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Location Data
The App requires location access to calculate accurate prayer times and qibla direction. Your location is **processed entirely on-device** and **is not sent to our servers**. You can revoke location permission at any time via your device settings.

### 2.2 Anonymous Usage Data (Firebase Analytics)
We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App. The data collected includes:
- Screens viewed
- Features used (e.g. Quran surahs opened, dzikir completed)
- Selected language, calculation method, and Arabic font
- App version, OS version, device model (anonymous)

This data **does not contain personally identifying information** and is used solely to improve the App.

### 2.3 Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
If the App crashes, we automatically receive a technical report (stack trace, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs. These reports **do not contain personal data or content you read/listen to**.

### 2.4 Advertising (App Open Ad — coming soon)
When the monetization feature is enabled, the App will display an **App Open Ad** (an ad shown when launching the app) using Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID to personalize ads. You can reset or disable Advertising ID via your device settings.

**Note:** The App does not display banner ads, native ads, mid-session interstitial ads, or rewarded ads.

### 2.5 In-App Purchases (coming soon)
If you purchase the Premium version or make a donation through the App, the transaction is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We only receive an anonymous purchase confirmation — we do not receive your credit card number or financial details.

## 5. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data **only on your device** (not uploaded to any server):
- Your prayer and dzikir history
- Surah and ayah bookmarks
- Settings (language, font, calculation method)

This data will be deleted when you uninstall the App.
